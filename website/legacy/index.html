A day in Streak takes a few minutes.

The app is built around one question you answer every evening: did I keep my promise today? Everything else is there to make the answer yes more often.

1

Read today's lesson

Each morning opens with a short card on why habits slip, like procrastination, perfectionism or waiting for motivation, and one tiny way to get started anyway. Slide to begin and you're in.

2

Check off your habits

Your list shows only what's scheduled for today: daily habits, plus the ones you set for specific days. Habits for other days wait underneath with the next day they're due.

3

Let the day count

Keep at least half of today's habits and the day counts toward your streak. Today stays open until midnight, so a slow morning never breaks anything.

Miss a day without losing everything.

Every habit gets one freeze a week. Use it on a day you missed and the streak carries on. Each habit card shows the last two weeks at a glance, so you can see exactly where the gaps are.

Rooms: a few friends, the same habits.

Start a room for a challenge, a study group or a gym crew. When someone checks off a habit the room tracks, it shows up in the room's feed, and everyone can see who's done for the day.

  • Invite by username or code. Add friends by their @username, or share the room's 6-character invite code.
  • Set how long it runs. 7, 14, 30 or 60 days, a custom length, or keep it going for good.
  • Cheer each other on. React to a friend's check-in with a heart, a flame or a thumbs up.
  • Public or private. Private rooms are visible only to the people in them.

Small things that help.

None of these are the point. They just take friction out of the way.

Works without signal

Check off habits on a train or in a basement. Streak saves them on your phone and syncs when you're back online.

Levels as you go

Every check-in earns 10 XP, and every 100 XP is a new level on your profile. A quiet record of how often you showed up.

Icons that pick themselves

Name a habit "Read" or "Run" and Streak suggests a matching icon. You can change it any time.

Questions

Why is it an APK and not on the Play Store?

Streak is in its first release, so it's shared directly for now. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time; that's normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.

What counts as keeping my promise for the day?

Checking off at least half of the habits scheduled for that day. Days with nothing scheduled don't break your streak, and today isn't judged until it's over.

Do I need friends to use it?

No. Habits, streaks, freezes and the daily lesson all work on your own. Rooms are there when you want company.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to the You tab, then Account settings, then Delete account. It removes your account, habits and history, and rooms you own.

When is the iPhone version coming?

It's in development. There's no release date yet.
