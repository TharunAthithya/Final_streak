## Rules & Behavioral Constraints
- **Track Codebase Changes**: Whenever you make a change, add a new file, edit database schemas/functions, or update configurations, you **must** append a summary of the change to the `project_audit_report.md` file located at the root of the workspace. Include:
  - Date and time of change
  - Description of what was changed and why
